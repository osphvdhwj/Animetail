### 🚀 Flagship Project: Aniyomi Plus (Animetail)
An open-source Android powerhouse unifying media streaming, reading, and tracking across the globe:
* **🎬 Video Player**: Full-featured MPV video engine supporting local playback, intent-based opening, next-episode autoplay countdowns, and floating AniSkip intro/outro skip.
* **📖 Universal Reading Hub**: Support for all reading formats — **Manga (JP)**, **Manhwa (KR)**, **Manhua (CN)**, **Webtoons**, and **Light Novels**.
* **🌐 Universal Tracker Client**: Deep integration with 7+ world tracking services: **AniList, MyAnimeList (MAL), Kitsu, Shikimori, Bangumi, Simkl, and MangaUpdates**.
* **📅 7-Day Airing Calendar**: Live countdown timers, broadcast schedules, and instant-play streaming bridges.
* **🃏 Tinder Discovery Deck**: Swipe-card recommendation discovery with dynamic stamps and depth animations.
* **🎨 Material 3 Expressive UI**: Dynamic Monet theming, Bento statistics charts, and streamlined navigation.
